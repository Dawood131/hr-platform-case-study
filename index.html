Overview

What it is

A full HR recruitment platform used by recruiters to run their entire hiring pipeline: posting jobs, reviewing and shortlisting candidates, scheduling interviews, rating talent, and messaging candidates directly, all inside one dashboard. It supports English/French localization and role-based access across multiple companies, so a single recruiter account can operate across several organizations with different permission levels.

Breakdown

What's built into it

Seven modules make up the product. Each handles a distinct part of the hiring process.

01

Dashboard & Talent Rating

Tracks the hiring funnel (jobs, applications, interviews, hires) as live counts with change indicators. A talent rating system scores candidates after interviews and ranks them, giving teams a consistent way to compare candidates across interviewers.

  • Live hiring funnel and application trend charts
  • Post-interview talent scoring and candidate leaderboard
02

Job Management

Recruiters post and filter job listings, review the candidates for each job, and shortlist them against the skills and fields the role actually needs.

  • List and post jobs
  • Candidates and shortlisting tied to job-specific skills
03

Interview Management

Interviews move from scheduling to completion inside a calendar system. Recruiters set availability, schedule against open slots, and run video interviews in-app.

  • Schedule new interviews, calendar, and availability
  • Configurable interview types
  • In-app video interviews
04

Messaging

A dedicated inbox threads every conversation by job and candidate, with file attachments. It is kept separate from networking, since job conversations and general networking serve different purposes.

  • Job-linked, threaded conversations
  • File attachments within threads
05

Professional Networking

A standalone networking module where users discover people, manage connection invitations, and message their connections, working as a mini social network inside the platform.

  • My network, people discovery, and invitations
  • Messaging scoped to existing connections
06

Team & Company Management

A role-based permission system controls what each team member can see and do, across multiple companies. Template management handles the email and notification templates used for automated candidate communication.

  • Team members, roles, and permissions
  • Multi-company profile support
07

System Configuration

An admin layer with account and notification settings, localization (currencies, languages, translations for English/French), and lightweight CMS screens for news, testimonials, and FAQs.

  • Account and notification settings
  • Localization and CMS
