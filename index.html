Overview

What it is

Connect Job is a full HR recruitment platform used by recruiters to run their entire hiring pipeline: posting jobs, reviewing and shortlisting candidates, scheduling interviews, scoring talent, and messaging candidates directly, all inside one dashboard. It supports English/French localization and role-based access across multiple companies, so a single recruiter account can operate across several client organizations with different permission levels.

Breakdown

What's built into it

Seven modules make up the product. Each was designed and built to handle a distinct part of the hiring process.

01

Recruiter Dashboard & Talent Rating

The dashboard tracks the entire hiring funnel, covering jobs, active jobs, applications, interviews, and hires, as live counts with percentage change indicators, so a recruiter can see momentum at a glance rather than digging through lists. Alongside it, a talent rating system scores candidates after interviews and ranks them on a leaderboard, giving recruiting teams a consistent way to compare candidates across different interviewers.

  • Live hiring funnel with jobs, applications, interviews and hires
  • Weekly/monthly application trend charts
  • Today's interview schedule surfaced directly on the dashboard
  • Post-interview talent scoring and candidate leaderboard
02

Job Management

Recruiters post new job listings, browse and filter existing ones, and move candidates through a shortlisting flow that's tied directly to each job's required skills and job fields, so shortlisting isn't a generic pass/fail step but is matched against what the role actually needs.

  • Post and list jobs with full detail management
  • Candidate shortlisting tied to job-specific skills and fields
  • Dedicated skills and job-field management screens
03

Interview Management

Interviews move from scheduling to completion inside a full calendar system. Recruiters set availability windows, schedule new interviews against open slots, and run video interviews directly in-app, with every interview type configurable rather than hardcoded.

  • Calendar-based scheduling with availability management
  • In-app video interviews
  • Configurable interview types
04

Messaging (Inbox)

A dedicated inbox threads every conversation by job and candidate, with file attachments supported per thread. It's built and kept fully separate from the networking system below, since a job-related conversation and a general networking message serve two different purposes for a recruiter.

  • Job-linked, threaded conversations per candidate
  • File attachments within message threads
  • Kept independent from the My Network messaging system
05

My Network

A standalone professional networking module, separate from recruitment itself. Recruiters and candidates can discover people, send and manage connection invitations, and message existing connections. It functions as its own mini-LinkedIn inside the platform rather than an extension of the job inbox.

  • People discovery and connection suggestions
  • Invitation sending and management
  • Messaging scoped to existing connections
06

Team & Company Management

A role-based permission system controls what each team member can see and do, and it extends across multiple companies, so an agency managing hiring for several clients can switch between company profiles without losing permission boundaries. Template management sits alongside it, handling the email and notification templates used for automated candidate communication.

  • Team member, role, and permission management
  • Multi-company profile support
  • Email/notification template management for automated messaging
07

System Configuration

Beyond day-to-day recruiting, the platform has a full admin layer: account and notification settings, localization covering currencies, languages and translations for English/French support, and lightweight CMS screens for News, Testimonials, and FAQs shown on the platform's public-facing content.

  • Account and notification settings
  • Localization: currencies, languages, translations
  • CMS for News, Testimonials, and FAQs
